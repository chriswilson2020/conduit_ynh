Mail works now: add a mailbox in Settings > Mail with its username and password. An account on your own IMAP server needs nothing beyond that, now or ever.

Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace are the exception, and they are experimental: no sign-in here has ever completed against a real Microsoft or Google account. Signing in at one needs a one-time app registration only an administrator of your own tenant can make, so Settings > Mail offers no provider buttons until one exists.

This app's page in the webadmin has the steps, the exact redirect URI to register for this install, and what has not been tested.
