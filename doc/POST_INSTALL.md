Mail works now: add a mailbox in Settings > Mail with its username and password. An account on your own IMAP server needs nothing beyond that, now or ever.

Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace are the exception. Signing in at the provider needs a one-time app registration made by an administrator of your own tenant, in Microsoft's or Google's console. Conduit cannot ship one for you, so Settings > Mail offers no provider buttons until one exists.

This app's page in the webadmin has the steps, and the exact redirect URI to register for this install.
